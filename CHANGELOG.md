# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (beta) — first release

For Minecraft 26.3, on Fabric and NeoForge.

- NPCs with skins, mob looks and custom Blockbench models; stats, gear and a working inventory.
- AI templates (guard, townsfolk, bandit, companion), wandering, patrol routes and AI rules.
- Factions with reputation, standings, rivals and guards that defend each other; a two-hit rule for accidental hits.
- Branching dialogue edited as a graph, with conditions and actions on replies.
- Quests with a builder, stages, objectives, rewards, quest cards, markers, a journal and a tracker.
- Animations (idle poses and emotes), particle auras and event effects, speech bubbles and sounds.
- A custom content library: upload sounds, particles, models and animations; the server sends them to players.
- In-game editor for everything, with tooltips and a (?) help mode on every screen.
